02 / RESEARCH

Research experience.

CURRENT RESEARCH

2026 – Present

San Francisco Bay University

Comparative Security Evaluation of Large Language Models

Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Satarupa Mukherjee, Ph.D.

I am working on a standardized evaluation of prompt injection and jailbreak attacks across seven LLMs and three datasets, including the BIPIA benchmark. The goal is to compare model behavior and examine attack success, task utility, and defense robustness using a reproducible evaluation pipeline.

My work includes literature review, preparing datasets and attack scenarios, running evaluations, analyzing results, and preparing a research manuscript. This project is ongoing; results are not yet reported here.
